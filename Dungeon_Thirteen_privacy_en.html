Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Dungeon Thirteen, including local play, online multiplayer, and Google Play Games features.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Summary. Local game data is stored on your device. Online multiplayer uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Google Play Games Services is used for leaderboards and achievements when the service is available on your device.

1. Data Controller

Giuseppe Maiello — Pin Soft House
Email: pinsofthouse@gmail.com

2. Information We Do Not Ask For Directly

Dungeon Thirteen does not ask you to create a separate in-game account or directly request your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or social-media account. Google Play Games sign-in, described below, uses the Google account and Play Games profile that you have already chosen to use on your device.

3. Local Game Data

The game stores gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as settings, preferences, and game progress. This information is used only to operate the game on that device. You can remove it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

4. Online Multiplayer and Firebase

Multiplayer is optional. If you choose to use it, Dungeon Thirteen uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google Firebase.

  • Anonymous authentication: Firebase creates a randomly generated anonymous identifier so the game can recognize a player during online play. It is not linked by the game to your name, email address, or a conventional user account.
  • Match information: Firestore is used to exchange the information needed to create and operate multiplayer rooms and matches, such as room and player state, game actions, and any display name you choose to enter. Do not use a real name or other personal information as a display name.
  • Technical information: As the service provider, Firebase may process technical information such as IP address and user-agent information to provide authentication, security, and abuse prevention. Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States; Firestore may process data on Google's global infrastructure depending on its configured location.

We do not use Firebase Analytics, advertising, or social-login features in Dungeon Thirteen.

Google's Firebase privacy information is available at Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Google Play Games Services

On Android devices, users who are already signed in to Google Play Games may be signed in automatically to Google Play Games Services. This optional Google service is used to submit and display leaderboard scores and to record and show achievement progress and trophies.

To provide these features, Google Play Games Services processes your Play Games profile and game-service data, which can include your gamer identity or profile name, player identifier, scores, achievements, and related app or device information. The game uses the information made available by the service only for its Google Play Games features. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy and its Play Games settings.

6. Purpose and Legal Basis

Local data is used to provide the game and preserve your settings and progress. Multiplayer data is used only to provide the online multiplayer feature you choose to use, including securing access to rooms and synchronising gameplay. Google Play Games data is used to provide leaderboards and achievements. Where applicable, this processing is necessary to provide the requested game functionality.

7. Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or profiling. Data used for multiplayer is shared only with Google Firebase as the service provider and with the other players in the same game where it is necessary to show the shared match state or a display name. Google Play Games data is processed by Google to provide its game-service features.

Local data remains on your device until you remove it. Multiplayer room and match data is kept in Firestore only for as long as needed to operate the online feature and maintain the game service; Firebase may retain service and security data according to its own policies. Leaderboard scores and achievement data are retained by Google Play Games Services according to Google's policies and your Play Games account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Dungeon Thirteen is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because multiplayer display names are visible to other players, users should not enter personal information in them.

9. Your Choices and Contact

You can choose not to use multiplayer, clear local game data through your device settings, or manage your Google Play Games profile and data through your Google account settings. Where applicable data-protection law grants you rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, you can contact us to make a request at pinsofthouse@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To help us locate an online multiplayer record, include the anonymous player identifier or room details only if you have them. Do not send sensitive information by email. Requests concerning your Google account or Play Games profile should be managed through Google account settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
